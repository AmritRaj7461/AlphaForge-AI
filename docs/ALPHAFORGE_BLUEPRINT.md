# Executive Summary

AlphaForge AI is an Explainable Investment Intelligence Platform that assists users in researching publicly listed companies using Artificial Intelligence.

Rather than acting as a generic chatbot, AlphaForge AI behaves as a structured investment research platform capable of collecting information from multiple trusted sources, reasoning over that information, and producing transparent investment recommendations.

The intelligence behind AlphaForge AI is powered by **ARGUS (Autonomous Research & Guidance Utility System)**.

ARGUS acts as the orchestrator of the platform, coordinating specialized research tools, aggregating evidence, validating outputs, and generating explainable recommendations supported by confidence scores.

AlphaForge AI is designed with a modular architecture that allows the current implementation to evolve naturally into a collaborative multi-agent ecosystem in future releases.

# Problem Statement

Investment research today is fragmented.

Users often switch between:

- Company websites
- Financial statements
- Annual reports
- Market news
- Analyst opinions
- Financial portals

This consumes significant time and still leaves uncertainty.

AlphaForge AI solves this problem by consolidating structured research into a single explainable report.

---

# Product Philosophy

AlphaForge AI follows one simple philosophy:

> Research First. Reason Second. Recommend Last.

The platform must never produce investment advice without sufficient supporting evidence.

Every recommendation should be explainable.

Every conclusion should reference the information used to reach it.

# Engineering Constitution

Every contributor to AlphaForge AI must follow these rules.

1. Blueprint is the highest authority.

2. Architecture before implementation.

3. Never bypass ARGUS.

4. Never expose API keys.

5. Backend communicates with external services.

6. Frontend consumes backend APIs only.

7. JSON everywhere.

8. Every recommendation must be explainable.

9. Fail gracefully.

10. Preserve extension points.
