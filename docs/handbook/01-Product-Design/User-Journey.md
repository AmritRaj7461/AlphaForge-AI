Landing Page

↓

## Step 2

Company Search

↓

## Step 3

Validation

System validates the company.

↓

Research

- Company Information
- Financial Data

- Risk Indicators
- Competitor Data

↓

↓

Report Generation

Dashboard displays

Company Overview

Financial Health

News Summary

SWOT

Competitor Analysis

Investment Recommendation

Confidence Score

↓

## Step 7

Follow-up Chat

User asks additional questions using report context.

↓

## Step 8

Decision

User makes the final investment decision.
