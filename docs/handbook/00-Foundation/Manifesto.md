# AlphaForge Manifesto

Investment research should not be fragmented.

Investment research should not require visiting dozens of websites.

Investment research should not depend on guesswork.

AlphaForge AI exists because information overload prevents investors from making informed decisions.

Instead of forcing users to manually compare annual reports, financial statements, earnings calls, analyst opinions, and news articles, AlphaForge AI performs structured research and presents findings in one explainable report.

AlphaForge AI is not built to replace investors.

It is built to assist them.

Artificial Intelligence should improve understanding—not remove human judgment.

Every recommendation generated by AlphaForge AI must remain transparent, explainable, and evidence-backed.

Research comes before reasoning.

Reasoning comes before recommendation.

Trust is earned through transparency.

That philosophy defines AlphaForge AI.
