# Design Principles

## Principle 1

Research before recommendation.

---

## Principle 2

Every recommendation must include supporting evidence.

---

## Principle 3

AI must communicate uncertainty.

---

## Principle 4

Graceful degradation is preferred over application failure.

---

## Principle 5

All services should be modular.

---

## Principle 6

Future scalability must not require architectural redesign.

---

## Principle 7

Every engineering decision should prioritize maintainability.

---

## Principle 8

Security, transparency, and explainability are mandatory—not optional.
