# Executive Summary

AlphaForge AI is an Explainable Investment Intelligence Platform designed to simplify equity research using Artificial Intelligence.

Unlike traditional chatbots, AlphaForge AI follows a structured research workflow.

Its primary AI system, ARGUS (Autonomous Research & Guidance Utility System), coordinates multiple research tools to collect company information, financial data, market news, competitor insights, and risk indicators before generating an investment recommendation.

The platform follows a simple philosophy:

> Research → Validate → Reason → Recommend

Every recommendation is supported by evidence and accompanied by a confidence score.

AlphaForge AI is intentionally designed as a modular platform.

Version 1.0 implements a single orchestrating AI agent (ARGUS) supported by specialized tools.

Future versions will evolve these tools into autonomous AI agents without requiring architectural redesign.

The long-term objective is to build an institutional-grade investment research platform that remains transparent, explainable, scalable, and trustworthy.
